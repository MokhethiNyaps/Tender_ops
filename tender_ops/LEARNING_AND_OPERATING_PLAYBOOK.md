# Tender Response Learning and Operating Playbook

Legal/rules snapshot: 7 October 2026. Recheck the law, the issuing institution's policy, the latest tender documents and every addendum for each live bid.

## The job you are learning

The client does not pay you to create the issuer's tender. The client pays you to turn an issued tender pack into a complete, accurate, evidence-backed bidder response.

Your work has five distinct parts:

1. Read and control the source pack.
2. Identify eligibility, functionality, preference, pricing and submission requirements.
3. Match each requirement to verified client evidence or an assigned action.
4. Assemble prescribed forms, technical material and approved pricing without changing the issuer's rules.
5. Prove completeness through independent QA and client sign-off.

Treat each requirement as the unit of work. A 1,000-page tender is then many traceable tasks that can run in parallel, not one giant writing task.

## Ten-day learn-while-earning sprint

| Day | Learn and practise on SC103 | Client-ready output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Separate issued documents from bidder-created responses; learn source hierarchy and addenda control. | Source manifest, tender snapshot and missing-document register. |
| 2 | Convert every instruction into a requirement with source, owner, evidence, deadline, response location and fatality. | Compliance/requirements matrix and responsibility map. |
| 3 | Build a controlled company evidence vault with entity, issue/expiry date and proof source. | Evidence register and expiry dashboard. |
| 4 | Keep eligibility, functionality, preference and price as separate evaluation gates. | Bid/no-bid memo and evidence-backed score simulation. |
| 5 | Trace workbook inputs, formulas, caps, totals and VAT. Shadow a competent estimator/QS. | Pricing-input schedule and workbook QA report. |
| 6 | Draft only from verified scope, experience, staff and equipment facts. | Technical response skeleton and clarification list. |
| 7 | Map every signature, initial, witness, commissioner and authority requirement. | Prefilled but unsigned returnables pack and signature schedule. |
| 8 | Red-team contradictions, missing pages, unsupported claims, expiry, formulas and totals. | Correction log and independent QA report. |
| 9 | Rehearse filenames, editable/scanned media, copies, envelope, address, deadline and proof of delivery. | Submission manifest and dry-run package. |
| 10 | Complete one supervised live tender with explicit approval gates; record lessons. | Client sign-off, release certificate and post-mortem. |

Do not compress pricing, professional technical review, statutory evidence or signatures merely to meet an artificial turnaround promise.

## What you can sell now

### Tender Readiness Sprint

Sell this first. It is valuable and lower risk while you build experience.

Deliver:

- Source-pack inventory and hashes.
- Tender snapshot and deadline map.
- Mandatory-returnables matrix.
- Missing-document and missing-evidence request.
- Preliminary bid/no-bid risks.
- Clarification questions.
- Responsibility map and production schedule.

Quote a fixed fee by scope band, complexity and urgency, with a written assumption that the clock starts only when the complete issued pack is received. Do not price purely by page count.

### Evidence Vault Setup

Organise current CIPC, CSD, SARS, CIDB, COID, B-BBEE, banking, locality, references, completion evidence, CVs, qualifications, equipment, commitments and standard policies. Track entity, issuer, issue date, expiry date, source file and approved reuse.

### Assembly and Compliance QA

After supervised practice, sell verified form prefilling, indexing, bookmarks, evidence cross-references, compilation, final readback and delivery control. The client still approves facts and signs.

### Specialist-led services

Only include these when the named specialist approves the work:

- Rates, production, quantities, escalation, contingencies and margin: estimator/QS/engineer.
- Construction methodology, temporary works, traffic accommodation, OHS and environmental plans: competent technical professionals.
- VAT, turnover, solvency and tax: accountant or tax practitioner.
- Contract qualifications, JV/subcontract terms and legal disputes: procurement/construction lawyer.
- Sworn affidavits and B-BBEE evidence: bidder, commissioner and/or authorised verification professional as applicable.

Never market a guaranteed award. Market a controlled, traceable and compliant process.

## Minimum client intake

Do not begin full production until you have:

- Complete issued pack, drawings, briefing material and addenda.
- Closing date/time, briefing rule, submission channel, copy/media rules and delivery details.
- Legal entity details and authorised-signatory evidence.
- Current CSD, SARS, CIDB, COID, B-BBEE and banking evidence.
- Locality and ownership evidence relevant to stated scoring goals.
- Project references, completion certificates and contactable referees.
- Personnel, qualifications, licences and availability.
- Equipment ownership/hire evidence and availability.
- Current commitments, JV/subcontractor details and disclosures.
- Supplier quotations, rate assumptions, production assumptions, margin and VAT decisions.
- Written authority for portal access, account use or submission actions.

## Production system

```text
Immutable source pack
  -> page/sheet extraction
  -> validated tender facts
  -> requirement register
  -> evidence matching and expiry checks
  -> bid/no-bid and clarification gates
  -> controlled forms, narratives and pricing
  -> client/specialist approvals and signatures
  -> binder assembly
  -> independent QA
  -> locked release package, manifest and delivery proof
```

Automation should control file hashes, page counts, deadlines, evidence expiry, workbook formulas, totals, binder order, filenames and missing attachments. AI may classify requirements, identify possible contradictions and draft evidence-grounded text. AI may not invent company facts, experience, staff, equipment, ownership, turnover, rates or signatures.

## Release gates

A bid is not submission-ready unless all of these are green:

1. **Source gate:** full pack and latest addenda received; conflicts resolved in writing.
2. **Eligibility gate:** every pass/fail condition supported by current evidence.
3. **Fact gate:** every claim approved by the client and linked to evidence.
4. **Commercial gate:** estimator-approved rates, assumptions, caps, VAT and final total.
5. **Signature gate:** authorised signatures, witnesses and commissioning complete.
6. **Technical gate:** competent-person approval of technical, OHS and programme content.
7. **QA gate:** totals reconcile, every requirement has a response location, scans are legible, filenames/media/address/deadline are confirmed, and a second person has performed the final readback.

## Ethical and legal boundaries

Never fabricate, embellish or backdate experience, ownership, turnover, locality, staff, equipment, certificates or statutory status. Never copy signatures or stamps, sign for the bidder, commission an affidavit, collude, access CSD/SARS without authority, or hide a conflict in the source documents.

The Constitutional Court declared the Public Procurement Act 28 of 2024 invalid on 17 September 2026. Its judgment described the 2022 PPPFA Regulations as currently in force. The rules engine must therefore keep a dated legal snapshot instead of assuming that an old template remains correct.

## First live-job rule

Use a current tender and a willing client as a supervised production run. Sell the readiness sprint first, issue the evidence request immediately, and expand the scope only after the bid/no-bid and specialist gates are satisfied. Record time spent per requirement and every rework cause; that data becomes the basis for future pricing, templates and automation priorities.
